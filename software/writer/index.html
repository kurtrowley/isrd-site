ISRD Writing Studio

A studio for learning to write well, and for big, complicated pieces of writing.

What it is

The Writing Studio is a place to learn to write well, and to do big, complicated pieces of writing: a book, a report, a proposal, a course. It is built on the Reader-First method, which starts from the person who will read the piece and works back to the page. It takes a manuscript from first idea to a developed, reader-tested draft, for fiction, nonfiction and instructional work.

What you do in it

  1. Start with a note and collect your sources.
  2. Map how the parts of the piece connect.
  3. Draft in your own words, one small step at a time, with help at each step.
  4. Test the draft with the reviewer, and revise.

Notes, sources, maps, drafts and versions stay together in one project.

What you leave with

A finished piece, the sources behind it, and a picture of how it fits together.

How the AI is used

The writing stays yours. When the AI offers words, they are a sketch for you to test and rewrite. The studio is built around the structure of expert writing (clarity, tension, structural revision, voice), with the AI helping at each step of that process.

Who it is for

Writers and other creatives, researchers and scientists, educators, consultants, and anyone with a long piece of work and too many pieces to hold in mind at once.

The Workspace (beta)

A newer layout puts a whole project on one screen: tasks on the left, the rendered page in the middle, and the assistant on the right, with built-in help. It uses the same sign-in as the Writing Studio.
