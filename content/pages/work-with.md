# Work with ISRD

We build with a small number of customers: a person or an organization that leads an issue and wants a project of their own, or a team that wants a setup for their field. We also work one-on-one with people who handle complicated problems for a living and find a systems approach hard to use.

Every project begins with a conversation about the problem you are trying to hold together. [Write to start one](mailto:kurt@isrd.com).

Each project is also a chance to learn whether the tools work, and we ask permission to include it in our research. You can say no, and it changes nothing about the work.
