Interdisciplinary Systemics R&D

A creative lab for the systems that hold a civilization together.

ISRD builds research, tools and publications on one idea: take a systems approach, in the modern sense that includes complexity and the human organism, to every domain that has to connect for humanity to become sustainable.

Our aim is a civilization where everyone can contribute: AI can give every person a voice in how Earth's resources are managed and where we go next.

Mission

Everyone gets a voice

AI can give everybody a voice: a say in how Earth's resources are managed and where our civilization goes next.

ISRD aims to help many producers, especially people who need AI support to be heard, and to keep the picture pluralistic: many perspectives, understood systemically.

Approach

Systemic by design

The same few ideas run through everything ISRD makes.

  • Start from the wholeMap the system, its feedback loops and its edges before changing any part of it.
  • Design for the person on the other endThe reader, the learner, the patient or the citizen is the system's other half, and the design target.
  • Test itModels you can run, drafts you can check, and results treated as data when they surprise us.
  • Keep people in chargeAI proposes and people decide. Tools scaffold judgment at every consequential step.
