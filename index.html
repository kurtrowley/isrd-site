Mission

Everyone gets a voice

AI can give everybody a voice: a say in how Earth's resources are managed and where our civilization goes next.

ISRD aims to help many producers, especially people who need AI support to be heard, and to keep the picture pluralistic: many perspectives, understood together.

Divisions

One lab, many fields

Approach

How we work

The same few ideas run through everything ISRD makes.

  • See the whole pictureMap the connections, feedback loops and edges of a problem before changing any part of it.
  • Design for the person on the other endThe reader, learner, patient or citizen is the other half of the system and the design target.
  • Test itModels you can run, drafts you can check, and results treated as data when they surprise us.
  • Keep people in chargeAI proposes and people decide. Tools scaffold judgment at every consequential step.
