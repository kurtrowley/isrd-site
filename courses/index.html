Readings

Complexity Science

  • Complexity Science
    Complex adaptive systems, emergence, self-organization, the edge of chaos, and attractor landscapes — from the Santa Fe Institute tradition.

Cybernetics

  • Cybernetics
    The science of control and communication in systems — feedback control loops, first and second order cybernetics, and how systems regulate themselves.

General Systems Theory

  • General Systems Theory
    Bertalanffy's interdisciplinary study of systems in general — boundary, wholeness, hierarchy, isomorphism, and equifinality as the ontological foundation of systems science.
  • Module 1: Foundations and Core Concepts
    What is a system, boundary and environment, open versus closed systems, wholeness, and equifinality.
  • Module 2: System Properties and Behavior
    Hierarchy, feedback, nonlinearity, and isomorphism — the GST toolkit for analyzing system behavior.

Systems Dynamics

  • Systems Dynamics
    Forrester's method for modeling dynamic systems — stocks, flows, causal loop diagrams, and the classic models that built the field.

Systems Practice

  • Systems Practice
    An integrated toolkit — diagnose, model, intervene, evolve — for applying all four foundation theories together across any domain.

Systems Science 101

  • What Is Systems Science?
    Systems science is the study of pattern — the structures and principles that recur across every domain, from feedback loops to attractors to emergence.
  • Feedback Loops
    The circular causality that makes system behavior counterintuitive — reinforcing and balancing loops, delay, and why feedback is the engine of dynamic behavior.
  • Emergence
    Why the whole exceeds the sum of its parts — attractors, hierarchy, and self-organization as the mechanisms behind emergent behavior.
