Reader-First Writing System

The cognitive and systems basis for reader-centered writing

The cognitive and systems basis for reader-centered instructional writing: designing documents that transfer understanding. The program treats the reader as the system being designed for, and builds a method for long-form writing around the reader's evolving state, expectations and motivation.
