Complex systemics research

Research reports

  • Designing Toward a Theory: An AI-Assisted Writing System as Design Experiment
    A case study of a thirteen-month design experiment in applied systems science: modeling the cognitive structure of expert long-form writing and building an AI-assisted environment — Systemic Writer — scaffolded around that structure rather than around text generation.
  • Regulatory Capture: How Closed Feedback Loops Get Stuck
    Sets a proposed ME/CFS mechanism (sustained cortical override of the respiratory pacemaker) alongside the same bistability, feedback, and sensitive-dependence patterns already illustrated through flocking and the Lorenz attractor — and introduces two new interactive demonstrations.
  • Energy Envelopes and Feedback Loops
    A systems-level explanation of why rest alone fails ME/CFS patients, and what the feedback dynamics of post-exertional malaise actually suggest about pacing as intervention.
  • Evolutionary Futurism
    Applies evolutionary and complex systems science to long-range civilizational questions — not prediction, but attractor mapping of where the trajectory is heading and where the leverage points lie.
  • Human Spiritual Systems
    Applies attractor analysis, feedback modeling, and network dynamics to why spiritual systems form, sustain themselves, and interact with health, governance, and knowledge systems — as structure, not theology.
  • The Attractor Engineering Framework
    Synthesizes five interdisciplinary traditions — General Systems Theory, Cybernetics, Complexity Science, Systems Dynamics, and Information Systems Theory — into nine core concepts across three tiers, from the ontology of complex systems to the operators of effective intervention.
  • ISRD Research Foundations
    Describes the research context underlying all ISRD work — five theoretical traditions in systems science, three domain research programs, and Kurt Rowley's two decades of prior published work in instructional design and educational technology.
  • Science & Religion: The Philosophical Divide
    A systems-theoretic examination of the structural conflict between scientific and religious epistemologies — identifying where the divide is genuine, where it is constructed, and where synthesis is possible.
  • Are We Alone? Systemic Evidence Synthesis
    Applies systems science methodology to evaluate empirical, theoretical, and anomalous evidence streams bearing on extraterrestrial life and intelligence with epistemic discipline, holding each stream to its own appropriate standard.
