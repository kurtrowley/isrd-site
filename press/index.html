ISRD Press

Stories and books, free to read

ISRD Press is the publishing house of ISRD. Its authors write with the ISRD Writing Studio, and every work is free to read.

Authors

Every author has a page of their own. It holds their biography, the works they have published, and the work in progress that they would like readers to see. An author can write under their own name or under a pen name.

How each work was made

Each work states how it was written, from written by the author with coaching, through shared writing, to drafted by AI and revised by the author. The label comes from the author's own record in the Writing Studio.

Read, and help authors improve their work

Anyone can make a free reader account. Readers follow authors, read early parts of a work, and leave comments that help the author revise. Finished works are open to everyone.

Opening soon

ISRD Press opens at press.isrd.com, and this page will link to it. To be told when it opens, write to kurt@isrd.com. Authors begin in the ISRD Writing Studio.
