About

Applied systems research and design, for people who change things

ISRD, Interdisciplinary Systems R&D, does applied interdisciplinary systems research and design. We take what the systems sciences and the sciences of each field already know, apply it to real problems, build tools for the people doing the changing, and find out in public whether the tools work.

Our aim is a civilization where everybody has a voice in how Earth's resources are managed and where we go next. AI can help many producers, and the people who need it most, be heard, so the picture stays pluralistic: many perspectives, understood together.

Our commitments

What we promise, and what we do not do.

  • People stay in chargeThe AI proposes and you decide. Consequential decisions stay with the people making them.
  • Sketches are labelledAI text meant for the page is called a sketch, kept apart from yours, and meant to be tested and rewritten.
  • We say how sure we areEvery claim is a known pattern, a likely parallel, or a guess worth testing.
  • We publish what we findIncluding when a tool did not help. We do not claim new theory about the nature of systems.
  • Customers publish their own workISRD provides tools and is not the publisher. We do not endorse what customers publish.

Mission

Everyone gets a voice

AI can give everybody a voice: a say in how Earth's resources are managed and where our civilization goes next.

ISRD aims to help many producers, especially people who need AI support to be heard, and to keep the picture pluralistic: many perspectives, understood together.

The researcher

Kurt Rowley, PhD

Systems scientist · Health researcher · Instructional designer

Kurt develops a rigorous, non-linear methodology for understanding complex adaptive systems, from post-viral illness to civilizational dynamics to the craft of writing. The work draws on general systems theory, cybernetics, complexity science and design-based research, and builds on decades of research in instructional design and learning technology.
