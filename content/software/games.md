# Bison Island Studios

## What it is

Bison Island Studios is a subsidiary of ISRD and the home of its games. ISRD also provides end-user products consistent with its mission, and games are among them. The first, a writing game, is in development now. It began inside the Writing Studio's workspace and now has its own site.

## What it is for

A practice game lets you try an expert skill on a small invented case before you do the real one. You make one move at a time and see what the consequences were. It is a safe place to build the skills that the Writing Studio then supports on real work.

## Who it is for

Writers and other creatives, students, educators, and anyone who would rather learn a method by playing through it first.
