Community

Planned.

Planned. Membership is not open yet.

ISRD is also meant to be a community of people who use these tools and want them to be better: applied systems thinkers, tool builders, project leaders, and people just getting started.

What members will be able to do

Share models, stories and methods; comment on each other's projects; say how well the tools work and what would make them better; and, in time, help choose which new features get built. Members who want to can be listed in a directory.

What membership is not

It is not a place for experts to talk over the heads of everyone else. It will run mostly in writing, at your own pace. Members will help look after it.

And later

A conference, once there is something worth meeting about.

If you would like to hear when membership opens, write to us.
