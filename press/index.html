ISRD Press

A student press: free books by writers who are learning

ISRD Press shares the work of people who write with the ISRD Writing Studio, a support environment for learning expert long-form writing. Every work is free to read.

A launch ramp for new writers

Writers learn long-form writing in the Studio with the game, the tutorial and the writing tutor, write their first few titles, and share them here for reviews. As their writing grows and their readership builds, they publish their best books in the wider world.

Read

Browse stories and books by genre, check one out, and read it online. Some are complete, and some are shared in parts while the author is still writing.

Help an author improve

Readers who join a beta leave comments that help the author revise. Authors read every comment, and a short note about where you kept reading or slowed down is a real help.

Authors

Every author has a page with a biography, their works and what they are working on. Authors write under their own name or a pen name.

How each work was made

Each work states how it was written, from written by the author with coaching, through shared writing, to drafted by AI and revised by the author.

Success stories

Books that began in the Studio and are now with publishers or on sale as self-published titles are listed under Success stories, with links to where to buy. ISRD Press publishes a few commercial titles of its own there, as one publisher among many.

Opening soon

ISRD Press opens at press.isrd.com, and this page will link to it. To be told when it opens, write to kurt@isrd.com. Writers begin in the ISRD Writing Studio.
