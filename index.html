Interdependent Systems Research and Development

Engaging and thought-provoking articles, simulations, and coming soon interactive simulation games that teach systems science.

ISRD is an applied systems science research and development firm. We conduct research designed to improve education, training, and productivity tools for anyone who wants to understand how complex interdependent systems can be understood and safely changed. ISRD specializes in evolutionary design experimentation.
