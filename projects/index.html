Projects

One issue, led by the people closest to it.

Climate has tools that let anyone try the options and see where they lead. Most other issues have nothing like it. Housing, health, safety, conflict, the limits to growth, consumer rights: each needs people who know the issue and its community, and a way to explore it together.

A project here is one issue, led by the people closest to it, using ISRD's tools. The public part is free to use. It links back to the tools, and across to other projects.

What a project gets

A model or set of models for the topic; a home page of its own; short primer pages in plain words for that issue; a path for a visitor (understand, explore your own situation, decide what you can contribute, act and write it up, share back); writing templates; and links to the other projects.

Start one with us

If you lead an effort on an issue and want this for your community, tell us about it. We will talk about what you need, what we can build with you, and what it takes to keep it going. Each project is agreed in writing: who owns what, who looks after it, how credit is given.

What we ask of a project

Models that carry a project's name are checked by someone who knows the field. They state their assumptions and how sure we are. ISRD provides tools and does not endorse a project's views.
