About

A creative lab for systemic research and products

ISRD, Interdisciplinary Systemics R&D, is a creative lab for systemic research and products. We apply a systems approach, in the modern sense that includes complexity and the human organism, to the connected domains humanity needs in order to become a sustainable civilization.

Our aim is a civilization where everyone can contribute: AI can give every person a voice in how Earth's resources are managed and where we go next.

How we work

The same few commitments shape every project.

  • Design as evidenceEvery project runs one cycle: map the system, form a testable hypothesis, build an intervention, and fold what happens back into the model. The working system is both the output and the evidence, and a visible failure is itself a finding.
  • Systemic synthesis firstThe parts of a system are nodes in a directed graph, linked by feedback. We model the connections before the components.
  • Modular by designEvery simulation is pluggable. A new model is a new configuration for the shared toolkit.
  • AI that supports expert judgmentComponents expose their state to AI services that interpret runs and suggest adjustments, while people make the consequential decisions.

Mission

Everyone gets a voice

AI can give everybody a voice: a say in how Earth's resources are managed and where our civilization goes next.

ISRD aims to help many producers, especially people who need AI support to be heard, and to keep the picture pluralistic: many perspectives, understood systemically.

The researcher

Kurt Rowley, PhD

Systems scientist · Health researcher · Instructional designer

Kurt develops a rigorous, non-linear methodology for understanding complex adaptive systems, from post-viral illness to civilizational dynamics to the craft of writing. The work draws on general systems theory, cybernetics, complexity science and design-based research, and builds on decades of research in instructional design and learning technology.
