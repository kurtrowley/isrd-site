Research program

Literary Systemics

Narrative structure as a feedback system

Applies systemic synthesis to the craft and impact of writing. Narrative is modelled as a feedback network: reader clarity, tension, and engagement are interdependent nodes. We develop a Reader-First framework that helps authors understand how structural choices propagate through the reading experience — and where interventions have the highest leverage.

Research questions

  1. How do reinforcing and balancing feedback loops manifest in effective narrative structure?
  2. Can systemic analysis predict reader drop-off, confusion, or emotional disengagement?
  3. What is the optimal tension-clarity trade-off for different reader objectives?

How AI is used

AI assists in generating structural critiques of narrative drafts, suggesting rebalancing interventions, and mapping text passages to feedback graph nodes.

Tools

  • Writing Impact Simulator
  • Narrative Feedback Loop Modeller
  • Author Strategy Analyser
